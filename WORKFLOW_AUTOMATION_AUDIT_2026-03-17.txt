========================================================

Report Date: March 17, 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
=================

Phase 1 delivered 10 core automation features achieving 63% overall automation (73% happy path, 30% error handling, 5% escalation). Phase 2 addressed failure modes and escalation gaps. With 8 of 12 Phase 2 features now implemented, the system has reached 80%+ automation, eliminating ~35 hours/week of manual work.

REMAINING GAPS: 4 Phase 2 features not yet started, plus inherent edge cases that require human judgment. See detailed breakdown below.
